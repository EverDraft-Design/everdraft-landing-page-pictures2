WRITER TRUST

Writer Ownership

Your work remains yours.

At EverDraft, we will never claim ownership of your stories, drafts, chapters, characters, worlds, ideas, or creative work.

EverDraft exists to give your writing a place to grow — not a place to surrender ownership.

Whether you are testing a new writing technique, exploring a plotline you are not sure will land, sharing early chapters, or preparing yourself for publication, EverDraft has been built with writers in mind.

Our promise is simple:

We aim to provide a safe, supportive space where writers can share drafts, gather thoughtful reader responses, build confidence, and begin finding the readers who care about their work.

Your words are yours.
We are simply here to help them find their first spark.
