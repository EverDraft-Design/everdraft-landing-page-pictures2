Testing Checklist

  1. Create an account at /signup/.
  2. Visit /account/ and edit profile details.
  3. Confirm your username is locked once it has been set.
  4. Open My Stories.
  5. Create a story at /my/stories/new/.
  6. Edit the story from the My Stories list.
  7. Open the story, add a chapter, and publish it when ready.
  8. Open the Library and test story follows, Sparks, and public reading pages.
  9. Leave a private Reader Note on a chapter from a second account if possible.
  10. Open Pinboard and confirm Reader Notes appear for the writer.
  11. Return to account, sign out, then sign back in.
