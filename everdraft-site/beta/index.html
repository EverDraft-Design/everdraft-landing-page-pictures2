A Short Test Flow

You do not have to post a polished story. A quick look around, a rough draft, or a note about what felt confusing is genuinely useful.

STEP 1

Create an account

Sign up, confirm your email, and check that you can log in.

Create your account Log in

STEP 2

Explore the Library

Visit the Library, open a story page if one is available, and check that the layout feels clear.

Visit the Library

STEP 3

Try a writer action

If you feel comfortable, create a story draft or test chapter. It does not need to be polished.

Create Story

STEP 4

Share what felt confusing

Use the feedback/contact option to tell me what worked, what broke, or what felt unclear.

Give feedback / Contact
