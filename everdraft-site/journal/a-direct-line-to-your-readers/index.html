EVERDRAFT NOTES

A Direct Line to Your Readers

September 21, 2026

Okay, so I have to level with you.

When I first dreamt up EverDraft, I kind of just built somewhere that I would feel safe posting my stories. And seeing others start to feel that same safety is absolutely mind-blowingly awesome.

I would like to thank the handful of you who have uploaded your stories, made an account, and read the stories already here. It doesn’t sound like much, but you have blown my expectations for this site out of the water and I am beyond chuffed. I have been distracted lately in my writing and have just checked in to find more stories, more accounts! It’s truly a lovely feeling.

What’s new?

Because I had a few messages on my Pinboard from users and I desperately wanted to reply, I have added the option of replying to messages on your Pinboard!! Of course, you don’t have to!! But if you receive feedback that is meaningful to you, now you have a direct line to your readers.

Also, this isn’t required. You can turn off messages to your Pinboard at any time. Want to just write and post? Go for it. Want to interact? Yup, I’ve got you. There is a new little notification tag that will light up if you have a new Reader Note waiting. Pretty cool, huh?

That leads me to… if you have used a feature on another site or have an idea that would be right up EverDraft’s alley, please don’t hesitate to get in touch.

Much love and happy writing/reading!!

Xxx
CJDuncan

P.S: I’ve run some optimisation for the site. Faster loading speeds etc. So click those sparks, read some chapters and show EverDraft’s writers some appreciation… if you want to :D
